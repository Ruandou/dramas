# DramaWave 全职编剧经 card check 加入 WGA（首份集体合同谈判日期未定）

抓取时间：2026-11-19 17:41（Asia/Shanghai）  
层：国外  
过门：合格（成本；WGA 官方新闻稿 + Variety + Deadline + Los Angeles Times，均已打开；合同条款未公布）

## 要点（已打开原文核对）

- WGA 官方新闻稿（标注 November 16, 2026，联系人 Sheva Diagne）：DramaWave 的编剧在成功完成 card check 后，与美国编剧工会（Writers Guild of America）组织在一起。公司现雇有 27 名编剧。组织工作始于 2026 年 7 月，8 月成立组织委员会，10 月 28 日向公司递交信件，要求承认 WGA 为集体谈判代表；公司迅速同意承认，待当天完成的 card check。编剧将开始准备谈判首份集体谈判协议，谈判日期尚未确定。
- Variety（2026-11-16 5:00pm PT，合 11-17 09:00 CST，记者 Tanya Fedak）与 Deadline（2026-11-16 5:00pm，记者 Katie Campione）独立核述同一新闻：这是微短剧公司中第一支获得工会承认的全职编剧队伍。谈判议题包括薪酬与福利、岗位保障、公平署名制度，以及对写作中使用 AI 的边界（Variety 用 parameters，Deadline 与 WGA 稿用 guardrails）。DramaWave 组织委员会成员、高级编剧 Peter Forbes，以及 WGA West 主席 Michele Mulroney 的表态在三份文本中一致。Deadline 写明消息由工会在 Monday 公布。
- Los Angeles Times（2026-11-16 6:08 PM PT，合 11-17 10:08 CST，记者 Cerys Davies）补充：多数编剧远程办公、主要在洛杉矶；称 DramaWave 为「Chinese company」；写片库「超过 30,000 部微短剧、18 种语言」。并称此前已有自由职业微短剧编剧就个别竖屏项目与 WGA 组织过，但全职编制是第一次。上述补充仅见 LA Times，不是 WGA / Variety / Deadline 已核口径。
- 对独立制作方：这是微短剧全职编剧首次进入 WGA 体系，后续合同可能改写出海平台的编剧成本、署名和 AI 使用规则；但首份合同尚未开谈，本轮没有任何费率、工时或 AI 条款文本，不能把「已承认工会」写成「已有新的最低片酬或强制禁用 AI」。

## 原文摘录（核对用，不全文转载）

### WGA 官方新闻稿（November 16, 2026）

- 「Today, the writers at microdrama company DramaWave have organized with the Writers Guild of America (WGA), following a successful card check.」
- 「DramaWave currently employs 27 writers. The organizing effort began in July 2026 and quickly gained momentum, as an organizing committee formed in August 2026. The writers delivered a letter to DramaWave demanding recognition of the WGA as their collective bargaining representative on October 28. The company promptly agreed to recognize the union, pending a card check that was completed today.」
- 「DramaWave writers will begin preparing to negotiate their first collective bargaining agreement. Bargaining dates have yet to be determined.」

### Variety（2026-11-16 5:00pm PT）

- 「The writers at vertical series platform DramaWave have organized with the Writers Guild of America, becoming the first full-time writing staff to receive union recognition at a microdrama company.」
- 「Through the newly formed union, the staff of 27 writers aims to negotiate workplace issues including compensation and benefits; job security; a fair crediting system; and parameters on the use of AI in writing.」

### Deadline（2026-11-16 5:00pm）

- 「Following a successful card check, the guild revealed Monday that the writers at DramaWave have organized a union to bargain over workplace issues such as compensation and benefits, job security, a fair crediting system, and guardrails on the use of AI in writing.」
- 「Bargaining dates for the first contract have not yet been determined.」

### Los Angeles Times（2026-11-16 6:08 PM PT）

- 「This marks the first full-time microdrama staff to be recognized by the Hollywood union.」
- 「Previously, freelance microdrama writers have organized with the Writers Guild for various vertical productions, but a full-time staff has never made this stride before.」
- 「DramaWave currently employs 27 writers. Its staff is remote, with a majority of the writers based in Los Angeles.」
