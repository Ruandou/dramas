### Detailed Content Summary per Episode

#### EP01: 新人入行前必看的创作指南 (Newcomer Guide)
**~8000 words | Published: 2025-09-08 | 8397 reads**

Key topics covered:
- **Short drama industry essence**: "Adult fairy tales" providing strong emotional points & immersion in ~3min vertical episodes
- **Audience**: ~7 billion users, mainly 31-50 age range, expanding to younger demographics
- **Revenue models**: Guaranteed fees (thousands to tens of thousands), profit sharing (up to hundreds of thousands), top-tier (million+ monthly)
- **Who suits short drama**: Web novel authors (strongest transition group), traditional screenwriters, zero-base newcomers
- **Mindset shift**: From "writer" to "product manager" — "novels face readers, short dramas face the market"
- **Practical guidance**: Script format standards, structure & rhythm design, dialogue polishing requirements
- **Training method**: "Three-step" drama analysis — beginner → advanced → expert level works
- **Common pitfalls**: Blindly following trends, not understanding platform needs, treating writing as sprint not marathon

#### EP02: 揭秘题材与IP选择的底层逻辑 (Theme & IP Selection)
**~8000 words | Published: 2026-01-16 | 4095 reads**

Key topics covered:
- **Three evolutionary stages of short drama themes**: From strong-emotion early period → micro-innovation transition → diversified quality era
- **Evergreen vs. high-concept themes**: Evergreen (urban romance, period legend) wins on completion quality; high-concept wins on differentiated innovation
- **IP adaptation vs. original**: IP has built-in fans but risks fan backlash; original has freedom but no safety net
- **IP selection golden rules**: Core innovation, emotional tension & fast pace, high visual feasibility, relatable characters
- **Newcomer practical path**: Scan rankings → practice imitation → choose themes that excite both you and audience
- **Three micro-innovation methods**: Character innovation (safest), background innovation, narrative perspective/emotion logic innovation
- **Pitfalls**: Value orientation issues, character-audience mismatch, excessive production difficulty

#### EP03: 如何打造吸睛的黄金开场 (Golden Opening)
**~8000 words | Published: 2026-01-16 | 3861 reads**

Key topics covered:
- **Why openings matter**: Three gates — screening selection, work quality floor, audience retention
- **Five essential elements**: Clear character establishment, clear main goal, strong predicament immersion, continuous emotional progression, effective information density
- **Structure framework**: 起(Start) → 承+转(Develop+Twist) → 合(Resolve with suspense)
- **Case comparison**: Mediocre opening (too faithful to source) vs. hit opening (extract core, reconstruct dramatic tension)
- **Six pitfalls**: Slow pacing, redundant dialogue, vague character, audience mismatch, empty emotions, lack of innovation
- **Self-check**: Can you convey "identity + predicament + goal" golden triangle in 1 episode?

#### EP04: 揭秘主线设计的底层逻辑 (Main Storyline Design)
**~6000 words | Published: 2026-01-21 | 1963 reads**

Key topics covered:
- **Main line as "spine"**: Provides tone-setting, serialization, and focus functions
- **From inspiration to main line**: IP adaptation ("gold mining & stringing pearls") vs. original development (growing from seed)
- **"Soul three questions"**: Can you explain story in one sentence? Can protagonist create instant empathy? Who is the target audience?
- **Practical outline writing**: Story synopsis → character profiles → key highlights; ~500 words per stage
- **Outline example**: 《替君语落阶》detailed breakdown with story background, characters, 4-stage plot, core suspense & highlights
- **Self-check & third-party review**: Verify goal clarity, stage progression, main line continuity
- **Pitfalls**: "Running account" outlines, subplot hijacking, survivor bias thinking

#### EP05: 揭秘短剧节奏的设计密码 (Rhythm Design) — *Initial Page*
**~6000 words | Published: 2026-01-21 | 6127 reads**

Key topics covered:
- **Rhythm essence**: Not just speed, but "emotional metronome" — managing audience's tension-relaxation-expectation cycles
- **Full-series rhythm (80-100 episodes)**: Golden window (EP1-10) → Development period (EP11-30) → Climax closure (EP31-80)
- **Single episode rhythm (1-3 min)**: First 30s (hook) → Middle 60s (substance) → Last 30s (cliffhanger)
- **Genre-specific rhythm**: Revenge (fast, fierce, precise), Sweet romance (moderate pace, emotional detail), Slow-burn premium (quality-driven, higher skill threshold)
- **Rhythm control trio**: Plot arrangement (conflict art), dialogue design (every word counts), character dynamics (relationship changes as catalyst), emotional control
- **Training methods**: "Subtraction" editing, targeted drama analysis
- **Pitfalls**: Ineffective "talk-fests", rhythm imbalance, blindly copying slow-burn hits, repetitive tropes

#### EP06: 期待、悬念、钩子设置的底层逻辑 (Expectation, Suspense & Hooks)
**~8000 words | Published: 2026-01-21 | 2749 reads**

Key topics covered:
- **"Soul triangle"**: Expectation (direction/trunk), Suspense (branches/leaves), Hooks (roots) — sequential logic matters
- **Building expectation**: Big expectation (ultimate goal by EP2) + small expectations (stage-by-stage milestones like game quests)
- **Suspense via information asymmetry — "Three-element theory"**: 4 models based on who knows what (protagonist/audience/supporting characters)
- **Hook design**: "Cut the climax in half" — end episodes at the moment before climax explodes
- **Hook types**: High-tension dialogue + impactful visuals creating "unfinished feeling"
- **Detailed case study**: 《我有八个姐姐》EP1-6 script with annotations showing expectation/suspense/hook integration
- **Pitfalls**: Hiding expectations, logic errors for forced twists, hooks disconnected from plot

#### EP07: 详解短剧情绪点设计的实操技巧 (Emotional Climax Design)
**~6400 words | Published: 2026-01-21 | 3414 reads**

Key topics covered:
- **Audience positioning**: "Achievement-oriented" vs. "Relationship-oriented" emotional needs; three male-audience segments
- **Pain point mining**: Male (safety/dignity collapse, can't protect family) vs. Female (emotional security breach, self-value denial)
- **Climax types**: Successful reversal (reasonable rise of underdog), face-slapping (unexpected yet logical), unexpected gains (avoid "unearned" feeling), CP sweet romance
- **Combination & innovation**: Multi-emotion-point combinations; updating traditional emotions (e.g., "equal partnership" romance)
- **Rhythm design**: Big emotional points every 5-10 episodes + small ones every episode; difficulty situation management (keep short, leave hope, targeted)
- **Pitfalls**: Emotional self-indulgence, logic gaps, punching at路人/wrong targets

#### EP08: 如何让你的剧本"更好过稿"并顺利落地 (Script Approval & Landing)
**~9000 words | Published: 2026-01-30 | 8462 reads**

Key topics covered:
- **Market evolution**: Users more diverse, producers more focused, platforms more pragmatic (landing feasibility now key criterion)
- **Safety line (compliance)**: Values/ethics, policy/law, expression scale — "compliance is baseline, not option"
- **Content quality**: First 3 episodes establish main line, clear non-deviating goal, memorable character tags, dense information rhythm
- **Execution feasibility**: Mental "budget accounting" (7-10 day shoots, limited budget), cautious use of high-difficulty elements, character design as key assessment
- **Character design principles**: "Useful + memorable" per character; core 3-5 people, max 20 total; each must serve plot/protagonist/info function
- **Self-check list**: Content → Landing feasibility → Completeness (3-step process)
- **"Lens-thinking" writing**: Before/after script rewrite examples showing literary→filmable conversion
- **Pitfalls**: Abstract "novelized" writing, "God's eye view" assumptions, ignoring production reality

#### EP09: 详解台词打磨的实战方法 (Dialogue Polishing)
**~6000 words | Published: 2026-01-30 | 3716 reads**

Key topics covered:
- **Dialogue as "blood and flesh"**: Concise yet information-dense ("compressed biscuits — small volume, big energy")
- **Forms**: Dialogue as primary, VO/OS as minimal supplements — "if dialogue can express it, never use voiceover"
- **Three core functions**: Push plot forward (primary), shape characters, control rhythm/plant foreshadowing
- **Genre-specific dialogue**: Historical/fantasy (era-appropriate language), Emotional (subtlety & rhythm), Comedy (natural humor from character/situation)
- **"Alive feeling" dialogue**: Observe real life, use dialects/catchphrases/industry terms judiciously, handle internet memes carefully
- **Self-check**: Match character/situation, read aloud & trim (cut 1/3 and see if core message survives)
- **Common mistakes with before/after examples**: Overly adult child dialogue, excessive OS, logic-disconnected emotional transitions

#### EP10: 网文作者转型实战指南 (Web Novel Author Transition)

- **Transition ≠ career switch**: Most authors do both simultaneously; 80-90% of short dramas are web novel IP adaptations
- **Advantages & challenges**: Strong story understanding vs. unfamiliar script format, "novelization" tendencies
- **Three key transformations**: Free-form → standard format, author perspective → audience perspective ("simple and direct"), long setup → instant conflict

- **Mindset management**: Expect rejection, maintain "empty cup" attitude, persistence through natural selection
- **Future trends**: Quality over quantity, micro-innovation (form, genre fusion, character depth)

### Key Insights for AI Drama Production Agents

1. **Structure templates**: 80-100 episode standard with 3-phase arc (golden window → development → climax closure)
2. **Single episode formula**: 30s hook → 60s substance → 30s cliffhanger
3. **Script format**: Standard notation including scene (日/夜, 内/外, location), character list, △ for actions, OS/VO sparingly, 闪回/闪出 for flashbacks
4. **Emotional rhythm**: Small emotional points every episode, big ones every 5-10 episodes
5. **Information asymmetry**: 4-model "three-element theory" for suspense design
6. **Character budget**: Core 3-5 characters, max ~20 total, each must serve clear function
7. **Production constraints**: 7-10 day shoots, limited locations, minimize VFX/crowd scenes
8. **Dialogue rules**: Concise, character-specific, push plot, no "water dialogue"
9. **Compliance**: Positive values, no legal errors, appropriate expression scale
10. **IP evaluation criteria**: Innovation, emotional tension, visual feasibility, relatable characters
