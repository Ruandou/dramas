## SCENE-004 · 瑶华阁（白莲居所）

- **名称**：瑶华阁
- **位置**：后宫东侧小院，白莲作为贵人的私人居所
- **时代**：架空朝代，唐宋风格
- **氛围**：清冷、孤立、温柔中带着哀伤——白莲在这里卸下所有伪装，是她唯一能做自己的空间。院子不大，一株老梅树，几丛竹，一池小荷。简朴但干净，像一个被精心隐藏的牢笼。
- **光线**：日间以侧窗天光为主，光线柔和。夜间仅油灯一盏，光线集中在书桌/窗边，其余空间暗淡。EP34兄妹相认场景：月光+荷花池反光，温柔金调。
- **色彩编码**：素白淡蓝（#F5F5F0 + #8FA4B8），辅以竹绿、梅枝灰
- **竖屏构图要点**：
  - 窗框景中景：白莲站在窗前望向远处宫墙的剪影
  - 梅花树下独处：人物在树下，花瓣飘落的纵向运动
  - 小桌特写：白莲写日记/整理思绪的私密空间
- **English AI Prompt**：
```
Yaohua Pavilion (瑶华阁), a small secluded courtyard residence for a low-ranking imperial concubine in an ancient Chinese palace, Tang-Song dynasty style. A modest but elegant single-story building with dark tiled roof, whitewashed walls, and wooden lattice windows. Simple rosewood furniture inside: a narrow bed, a writing desk, a bronze mirror, a folding screen with lotus embroidery. The space is clean, sparse, and lonely — a beautiful cage. Soft natural daylight through lattice windows (4500K), or a single oil lamp at night creating warm pool of light in an otherwise dark room. Atmosphere: isolated sanctuary, quiet melancholy, the only place the female lead can be herself. 9:16 vertical composition using window frames and garden elements as depth layers, cinematic, photorealistic, 8K detail.
```
